Book 1 is mostly about the road to Holmgard. The median character finishes it in 92.4% of games, from 81.3% to 98.4%, and on
the best road the only fight that kills is the Giak and its Doomwolf at section 361: every death of every solved character
happens there. The rest of the book is about not taking a road that leads to it badly hurt, or to a dead end.

- At section 1, take the right path into the wood.
- When attacked at section 85, evade by running south, deeper into the forest. At section 14, run over the hill rather than
  stand, and at section 106 follow the stream to the east.
- At section 264, keep going south.
- At section 78, tell him that you are a Kai Lord with an urgent message for the King, and at section 129 keep following the
  officer.

Healing and Camouflage are the most useful disciplines, about 3 and 2 points of the series each. Of the random starting item,
the Chainmail Waistcoat is clearly the best, at 66.4% against about 60% for the healing potion or the 12 Gold Crowns. The Gold
Crowns of the pouch hardly matter: 0 and 9 differ by less than a tenth of a point.
