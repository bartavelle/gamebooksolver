In Book 4, every character dies in about 10% of games, whatever it does: when you run for the sun-flag at section 225, one
roll in ten sends you to section 181, where a sniper's arrow kills you. The solver still takes that road, as the other way
past the bandits at section 307 is worse. Apart from that, the book is won by finding the right way at a handful of sections.

- At section 101, take the boat south, and at section 115 land at the steps to investigate the tunnel.
- At section 235, look over the low balcony, which is reached in nine games out of ten and lost without it.
- Keep going south at section 120, enter the hall at section 228, and examine the chest at section 257.
- Quicken your pace at section 307 and try to outrun the bandits.

What you bring matters more than in Book 3, because it is carried into Book 5: the Sommerswerd is worth about 5 points of the
series, the Potent Strength Potion 3 and the Silver Helmet 3. Characters without the Sommerswerd carry a Dagger instead, which
is why the Dagger shows as a loss of the same size.
