Book 3 is almost never lost in a fight: the median character finishes it in 100.0% of games, and the worst in 99.7%. What can
go wrong is getting lost or trapped in the caverns, and a single wrong turn there costs the whole series. That is why every
character, whatever its disciplines or kit, ends up within one point of the others, at about 80.6% for the rest of the series.

- Take the Viad Glacier route at section 1. It is worth 7 points on average over the longer road.
- At section 300, give up two days and go back to the Viad Glacier.
- In the passage of section 30, examine the door, then open it and pull the lever at section 203. Taking another way there
  loses the series for the characters that reach it.
- At section 254, open the cell door.
- Leave the Gold Bracelet at section 316.

Disciplines and starting items change almost nothing here: Healing is the most useful, at a tenth of a point.
