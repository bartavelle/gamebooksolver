Book 2 is decided before it starts: without Animal Kinship, it is close to impossible. Every way through the Tarnalin tunnel
meets a Helghast that only the Magic Spear can hurt, except the colony of Noodnics, which only a character with Animal Kinship
can follow. Characters without it win the series in 0.005% to 1% of games, and those with it in 60.6% to 71.4%: see R34 in
ASSUMPTIONS.md. The Helghast that guards the Spear at section 106 causes seven deaths out of ten.

- In the Tarnalin, with Animal Kinship, follow the Noodnic at section 279.
- At section 349, raise your hands and walk towards the wagons.
- At section 307, show the guards the Seal of Hammerdal, if you still have it.
- At section 26, attack the zombie captain, and at section 200, attack the merchant Halvorc.
- At section 315, put the box back and join the captain on deck before he suspects anything.
- At section 71, run up the stairs.

The falling mast at section 247 kills in about one game in ten. Starting items matter little: the Chainmail
Waistcoat is worth about a point. Starting gold helps only a little, 33.1% at 10 Gold Crowns against 33.8% at 20, and nothing
changes above 26 (G2b). These numbers are for 10, 20 and 50 Gold Crowns only.
