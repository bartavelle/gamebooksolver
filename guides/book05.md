# Book 5: Shadow on the Sand

Book 5 ends the series, so its numbers are only about this book. The median character finishes it in 83.5% of games, but the
range is wide, from 38% to 90%, and most of it comes from one discipline: Mindshield. Each solved character lacks exactly one
of six disciplines, and those lacking Mindshield average 68.5%, against 82.6% for the others.

- At section 204, with Sixth Sense, try to grab the stone and turn it on Haakon. Without Sixth Sense, grab it at section 335.
  This is reached in two games out of three and decides the book.
- Against the Drakkarim, keep fighting rather than evade.
- At section 253, drink a strength potion for the fight with the Dhorgaan: the Potion of Alether if you have it, otherwise the
  potent one. The Dhorgaan is the second cause of death, after the fall at section 261.
- Pick up the sword at section 138 when the guards open the door, and climb the ladder at section 164 in the sewers.
- At section 361, keep your potions unless your Endurance is 16 or less.

One death is out of your hands: on the skyship, at sections 275 and 336, one roll in ten makes you fall to your death at
section 261, about 10% of games. In kit, the Potent Strength Potion is worth about 8 points, the Sommerswerd 6, the Silver
Helmet 5 and the Chainmail Waistcoat 5. Characters without the Sommerswerd have a Sword, hence its loss of the same size.

Starting gold makes no difference from 20 Gold Crowns up, which every character playing the series has: see G5 in
ASSUMPTIONS.md.
