# Rocq proofs

A model of the rules of the five Lone Wolf books, and proofs that the optimizations of the Rust solver don't change a
result. `ASSUMPTIONS.md` at the root of the repository says what is proved and what isn't.

Build with `make` here. `petctl.py` steps through proofs without rebuilding, and `-R . LoneWolfProofs` lets a file require
another by its short name, whatever its directory.

- `base/`: nothing about the game.
  - `order.v`: decidable equality and total orders.
  - `mps.v`: maps, sets and bags as sorted association lists.
  - `lists.v`: facts about lists.
  - `proba.v`: distributions and their expected value.
- `model/`: the game. A result is only as good as these files, as they are what has to be trusted.
  - `chapters.v`: what a book is made of: items, flags, outcomes, decisions.
  - `character.v`: the state of a character.
  - `hits.v`, `combat.v`: the combat table and the fights.
  - `rules.v`: how a state goes through a chapter.
  - `value.v`: the expectimax of a book, which all the theorems compare.
- `books/`: the books, as data.
  - `book01.v` to `book05.v`: generated by `gen_books.py` from `json-chapters/`.
  - `books.v`: the books as played, with the rounds of the fights added.
  - `well_formed.v`: statements that the books are well formed, not proved yet and used by nothing.
- `proofs/`: the optimizations.
  - `fight_facts.v`: what a fight looks at, and where it can lead.
  - `simulation.v`: `Tracker` and `value_same`: states that a part of the game can't tell apart have the same value.
  - `flags.v`, `flag_books.v`: flags that a book doesn't read.
  - `gold_items.v`, `gold_relation.v`: gold beyond what is needed, as a `Tracker`.
  - `gold_retrieve.v`: getting the equipment back, which adds up two purses (c14 of Book 5).
  - `gold_needs.v`: the need of each chapter, and `gold_needs_sound`.
  - `gold_tables.v`: the needs of the books, generated by `gen_gold_tables.py`.
  - `fight_nonneg.v`: the chances of a fight without poison are never negative.
  - `fight_mono.v`: a plain fight is never worse with more endurance or a better ratio.
  - `gold_more.v`: more gold is worth at least as much, on a book that only asks if there is enough of it.
  - `proba_books.v`: in every random outcome of the five books, no chance is negative and they add up to 1.
  - `gold_phases.v`: the chapters of Book 5 before and after the equipment is stored, generated by `gen_gold_phases.py`.
  - `gold_books.v`: the tables checked on the books, what follows for Books 3 and 4, and that more gold is never worse in Book 5.
  - `valid_check.v`: what a book has to respect to keep a state valid, computed on the five books.
  - `valid_items.v`, `valid_fight.v`, `valid_state.v`: what keeps the items valid, that fights never give endurance, and
    the effects on a state.
  - `valid_play.v`: `play_keeps_valid`: playing the five books keeps a state valid.
  - `no_overflow.v`: which fights double a kill while played round by round.
  - `cartwheel.v`: staking one crown is optimal at the Cartwheel.
  - `weapon_analysis.v`: the analysis of `WeaponUse::for_character`, the book specialised for the disciplines and the
    weapons that each chapter names, as in the Rust.
  - `weapon_swap.v`: states that only differ by the exchange of two weapons, and what the rules do to them.
  - `weapon_value.v`: `weapon_swap_value` and `weapon_rename_value`: renaming the plain weapons doesn't change a value.
  - `weapon_books.v`: Books 1 to 4 never give the equipment back, so that the table of the Rust is enough there.
- `difftest/`: tests on the extracted model. `run.sh` runs it against the Rust solver on random characters, and `valid.sh`
  checks on the books that playing keeps `ValidState` true. `evasion.sh` checks the fights of Book 4 that can be evaded against
  a separate calculation, which reads the combat table from the XML of the book.
